In my third year I started reaching out to seniors. They told me what specific companies were looking for, and shared what they had seen in the rooms I was about to walk into.

When EXL Analytics came to campus, 180 of us sat the written test. About 90 made it through. After three rounds of interviews, they picked 13. I was one of them.

I was not a top-ranking student. I was a prepared one. My seniors had told me EXL cared about guesstimates and puzzles, so that is what I practised. Everyone worked hard. My preparation was simply pointed at what the company valued.

That is the whole idea behind UnsaidTalks. I call it **information arbitrage**: the fastest way to learn is from someone who walked the exact same path recently. I got hired because seniors told me what nobody else would. Everything I've built since is about giving that to students who don't have a senior to ask.
