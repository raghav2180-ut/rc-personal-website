Nobody around me was building startups. I learned what I could from TVF's *Pitchers*, YouTube videos and whatever I could get my hands on.

My first idea was **Campus Kiosk**: a marketplace where seniors could sell their used books, drafters and notes to juniors. OLX, but only for our campus. I set up a stall, printed posters and pitched anyone who walked past.

Campus Kiosk never became a company. But the itch it came from never left.

On 24 November 2016, in my final year, I launched something smaller: a WordPress blog for the juniors in my club, answering the questions they kept asking me. I called it **UnsaidTalks**.
