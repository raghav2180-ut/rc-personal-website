I graduated in 2017 with a B.Tech in Electronics and Communication, joined EXL as a business analyst, and ended up leading the Python automation for financial data from thousands of US insurance companies.

UnsaidTalks stayed alive on the side. Through our campus ambassadors, juniors met the seniors who had just cracked placements and wrote down what they were asked. That became a library of **1,100+ interview experiences** across 7+ colleges and 50+ companies, free on our website. In placement season, the site drew 25,000+ visitors.

People noticed before I expected them to. Vinay Kumar sir started telling his students about UnsaidTalks whenever he talked about interview preparation. In 2018, with his help, the ECE department at Thapar, my own department, signed an MoU with what was still a student blog.

In 2020 we ran our first two-week mentorship program. It was small, and it was the start of everything that came later.
