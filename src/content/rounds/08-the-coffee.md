By 2024, my wife **Kajol** had joined UnsaidTalks full time, running it with a few interns while I kept my job. I loved my work at EasyInsights, and the fear of leaving it held me back.

In October 2024, two things happened in the same month. We ran our first college cohort, a pilot of the UnsaidTalks Mentorship Program at Manav Rachna (MRIIRS). And I noticed Dr. Amarjeet Singh writing on LinkedIn about the exact gap we were trying to close.

I hadn't spoken to him since leaving Zenatix. I sent him one message, asking for feedback on what we were building. He asked me to come and meet him.

That coffee with Amarjeet was the turning point of my life. He met our mentors and understood the programs we were running. What we had built was still very small, but he saw something in it. Then he asked me a simple question: why wasn’t I doing this full time?

I told him the truth. I didn’t have the courage to leave my job. Kajol was already full time on UnsaidTalks, and with neither of us drawing a salary, life would get hard.

He told me to go home and work out how much money it would take for me to leave my job and focus on building the company.

I cried that day. Just like that, I had the final push I needed, and the belief of someone ready to back us. Our second meeting locked his angel investment.

When I told our mentors I was going full time, they didn’t just cheer. **Rohan Gupta** (Senior Design Engineer, NVIDIA), **Akshit Arora** (Senior Data Scientist, NVIDIA), **Naman Rastogi** (Senior Manager, Design Verification & Emulation, Sandisk), **Pratibha Ola** (GPU Physical Design Engineer, Apple) and others put in their own money. The people who had watched me work with students for years were the ones who backed me. It gave me the confidence that people could see something in me, and that I was on the right track.
