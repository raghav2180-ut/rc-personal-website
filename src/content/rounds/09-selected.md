On 19 March 2025, after nearly a decade of building it alongside full-time jobs, I went full time on UnsaidTalks.

Ten angel investors backed us, led by Dr. Amarjeet Singh. In January, the CSE department at Manav Rachna had signed an MoU making us their permanent mentorship partner.

That partnership happened because of people who cared as much as we did. **Dr. Divyanshu Sinha**, then a professor at MRIIRS, shared our belief in making students industry-ready and ran our cohorts with us, making sure every student took the sessions seriously. **Ms. Meghna**, placement coordinator and professor in the CSE department, did the same, and **Dr. Tapas Kumar**, then head of the department, recognised the work and made the MoU happen. Later, Dr. Divyanshu wrote this about us:

> “Through structured interview preparation, insightful mentorship sessions, and valuable industry exposure, UnsaidTalks has played a pivotal role in significantly increasing placements at our university. Seeing our students successfully transition into their professional careers is a testament to the effectiveness of this program.”
>
> **Dr. Divyanshu Sinha**, educator in technology, formerly at MRIIRS

Kajol and I had no other source of income. People in our network told us, kindly, that at least one of us should get a job. We took a co-working space because we didn't want to build it from home. Our interns worked remotely. In the first six months, our mentors helped 50+ students prepare for the jobs they wanted.

Some days it felt like we were going to change the world. Other days the uncertainty hit hard. I learned that building a startup is an internal war, with your own fears and doubts, more than an external one.
