For more than a year, Amarjeet and I kept coming back to the same two questions. How could we make a deeper impact on a student’s life? And how could we stay with students for three or four years, the length of a degree, without opening a college?

The answer is **UnsaidTalks School of Technology F.U.E.L.** (Focus, Upskill, Earn, Learn). It was Amarjeet’s brainchild: a program that merges college time with real industry work, for students pursuing the IIT Madras BS degree online.

In 2026 we held our first offline event, at IIIT-Delhi, for IIT Madras BS students. Our mentors sat on a panel debating the pros and cons of online education. When we opened admissions, more than 200 students applied in 15 days.

In October 2026 the first F.U.E.L. batch started. Fourteen students study for their IIT Madras BS degree from our office and spend a fifth to a third of their time with our tech team and mentors. We don't treat them like college students. We treat them like fresher interns: a personal roadmap, their own Kanban board, daily stand-ups and, when they are ready, work on live client projects.

Content is a solved problem. With AI, anyone can learn anything. What's still hard is knowing what to learn, how much and how. And some skills only come from doing the work around people who do it every day: explaining your thinking, taking feedback, working in a team.

Our bigger bet is a distributed college, without being a college. Students who train like engineers from year one won't graduate as freshers. They'll walk out working like experienced engineers.

We're early and still learning. Always Day 1.
