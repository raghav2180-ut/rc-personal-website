title: "The last bench"
asked: "Can a shy kid who has never spoken in public ever stand in front of a room?"
tip: "the senior you admire is closer than you think. message them."
---

My first few weeks at Thapar, I sat at the last bench of a lecture hall and waited. A final-year senior, Naman Rastogi, was about to speak. He was General Secretary of the IETE society and had just been placed at SanDisk.

He talked about his interviews candidly, as if he were letting us in on something nobody else would tell us. I had never spoken in front of an audience in my entire school life. Sitting there, I pictured myself doing the same thing for my juniors one day.

By my final year, I had. More than once.

Ten years later I found Naman again and thanked him for being my first inspiration at college. Today he mentors UnsaidTalks students, and he is one of the people who invested in it.
