The first picture is when Kajol and I decided to take a co-working space because we didn't want to work from home while building UnsaidTalks Education.

We had a few interns working remotely and were still figuring things out.

The second picture is from September 2026, exactly 1.5 years later.

A new office. More Team Members. All of us working full-time, all offline.

The last 1.5 years have been nothing less than a dreamy ride.

But what most people don't see are the 7 years before that.

From many years, I ran UnsaidTalks part-time alongside various roles I took up in multiple companies. I kept taking decisions that many people around me didn't understand, including some of the closest people in my circle.

Those 7 years built the foundation for UnsaidTalks, but more importantly, they built me.  
The faces you see here are of me, Kajol and our amazing team.

But behind these faces are our UnsaidTalks Mentors, our key advisor Dr.Amarjeet, all our advisors and investors, and many others who believed in us and helped us get here.

7 years of building quietly. 1.5 years of building together.

And this still feels like just the beginning. Always Day 1... ❤️
