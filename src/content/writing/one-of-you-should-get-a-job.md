6 months of building UnsaidTalks Education, full-time, with my wife Kajol Chopra by my side.

We have no other source of income apart from UnsaidTalks Education, many people in our network told us - “At least one of you should get a job.”

But something deep inside me says, we’ll make this work.

UnsaidTalks isn’t just a company for me, it’s my passion turned into purpose.  
I never started it with profits in mind. The intent has always been simple - to help students in tier 2 and tier 3 engineering colleges who face the same confusion and lack of guidance that I once did.

With the support of our incredible mentors, we’ve already helped 50+ students prepare for their dream jobs in last 6 months and their heartfelt testimonials keep us going.

The journey so far has been full of ups and downs...  
Some days, it feels like we’re going to change the world.  
Other days, the uncertainty hits hard, will we even be able to scale this?

I’ve realized something powerful: Building a startup isn’t an external battle. It’s an internal war.  
A fight with your fears, doubts, and insecurities.

The day we conquer those... success will follow.

I hope to prove everyone wrong - the ones who doubted us, and perhaps even the voice inside me that still does sometimes.

Here’s to the journey - uncertain, imperfect, but ours. 💛
