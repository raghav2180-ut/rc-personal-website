If I could sit down with my 21-year-old self for just 10 minutes, this is what I'd say.

1. Stop worrying that you're falling behind.  
You're not. Life isn't a race. The only comparison you have to do is with your yesterday self.

2. Your career won't be a straight line.  
Business Analyst -> Software Developer -> Sales -> Marketing -> Growth -> Ops  
You'll experience all and think you're changing directions.  
In reality, you're collecting skills that will one day fit together perfectly.

3. Don't optimize only for salary.  
Optimize for learning. You will realise that sometimes choosing the right people over higher salary will give you way more return in coming years which you can't even imagine.

4. Create more than you consume.  
Write. Speak. Build. Share.  
You have no idea how one post, one video, or one conversation can change someone's life - or your own?

5. Learn to hear "No" without doubting yourself.  
Your closest people will say NO to your ideas. Clients will say no. People will question your decisions. Keep showing up. Every NO is helping you build resilience.

6. Your biggest breakthroughs will come after your hardest years.  
There will be days when nothing seems to work. When you'll question every decision you've made. Don't quit during those days. That's usually when everything is about to change.

7. Choose mentors, not idols.  
One person who genuinely believes in you can save you years of confusion.  
Stay close to people who make you think bigger.

8. Don't wait for permission to build.  
Nobody is going to tap you on the shoulder and say,  
"You're ready now."  
Start Today without waiting for right time in future.

9. Bet on yourself before the world does.  
One day you'll leave the comfort of a stable career to build something that exists only in your head.  
People will call it risky.  
Years later, they'll call it courage.

10. Remember why you started.  
It was never about titles.  
Or funding.  
Or LinkedIn likes.  
It was about creating opportunities you wish someone had created for you.  
Don't lose that.

One last thing...

You'll spend years trying to find the "right" path. Eventually, you'll realize there isn't one. The path appears only after you start walking. Keep walking.  
One day you'll look back and realize...

Every rejection, every career switch, every late night, every doubt was preparing you for the life you're living today.

And you'll be grateful you didn't quit. ❤️... more
